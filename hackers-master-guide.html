01.

About the Guide

Most security books teach one side — attack or defense. The Hacker's Master Guide teaches both, in the order an operator actually learns them, so every offensive technique is paired with the detection and defensive counter that follows it. It's written to be read cover to cover or used as a lab-side reference, and every concept is grounded in tools you can run today on Kali, Parrot, or BlackArch.

02.

What's Inside

Across its seven volumes, the guide covers:

IRecon & Enumeration

Passive and active reconnaissance, OSINT, scanning, and service enumeration — building the target picture before you touch a thing.

IIWeb & App Exploitation

The OWASP landscape in depth — injection, auth flaws, XSS, SSRF — with hands-on methodology drawn from real challenge sets.

IIIExploitation & Payloads

From vulnerability to foothold: exploit workflows, payload crafting, and safe, lab-scoped delivery.

IVPost-Exploitation & AD

Privilege escalation, lateral movement, and Active Directory attack paths — plus the artifacts they leave behind.

VEvasion & OpSec

Living-off-the-land, obfuscation, and operational security mapped to modern MITRE ATT&CK tradecraft.

VIDetection & Defense

The blue-team half: logging, EDR, SIEM, and how each attack in the book is caught and stopped.

VIITooling, Automation & Reporting

Scripting your workflow in Python and Bash, and turning findings into professional, repeatable reports.

+ Labs & Missions

Guided, authorized-only exercises throughout — the practical backbone that ERR0RS-Ultimate turns into interactive missions.
