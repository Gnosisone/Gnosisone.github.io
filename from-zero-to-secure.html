01.

About the Guide

Most security resources assume you already know the jargon. From Zero to Secure doesn't. It starts at the very beginning — what security actually means, why it matters, and how the pieces fit together — then builds, chapter by chapter, into the practical skills you need to start doing the work. It's the guide I wish I'd had on day one.
