01.

About the Book

Metasploit is the backbone of a huge amount of red-team work — but the framework is deep, and the gap between "I ran a module" and "I understand what I'm doing" is wide. This book closes that gap: a hands-on tour of the Metasploit Framework and Armitage that focuses on repeatable, professional workflows you can trust in an authorized engagement or a home lab.
