01.

About the Guide

Skill gets you in; OpSec gets you out. This guide treats operational security as a craft in its own right — a set of habits, tools, and decisions that protect the operator, the client, and the engagement. It's written around a simple rule that runs through every chapter: assume you're being watched, and behave accordingly. Every technique here is framed for authorized testing and client safety, never for evading legitimate accountability.
